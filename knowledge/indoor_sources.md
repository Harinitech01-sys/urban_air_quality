# Knowledge Base: Indoor Air Pollution Sources & Zero-Cost Hacks

## 1. Major Indoor Emission Culprits
- **Incense (Agarbatti / Dhoop)**:
  - Generates benzene, formaldehyde, and ultra-fine carbonaceous particulates in concentrations often exceeding outdoor smog (indoor PM2.5 > 500 µg/m³ within 15 minutes).
  - Substitute with LED diyas or flameless aromatics during severe smog episodes.
- **Dry Sweeping (Jhadu)**:
  - Resuspends settled coarse and fine particulate matter, including toxic heavy metals and brake dust tracked in from outdoors.
  - Substitute strictly with **wet mopping** (using water or mild detergent) and microfiber dusting.
- **Unvented Space Heating (Angithi / Coal / Kerosene)**:
  - Dangerous combustion without chimney vents leads to lethal Carbon Monoxide (CO) poisoning and nitrogen oxides (NO2).
  - Use oil-filled electric radiators or radiant halogen/ceramic heaters instead.
- **Cooking Oils & Frying**:
  - Deep frying and spicy tempering (tadka) aerosolize oil droplets.
  - Run exhaust fans on high throughout cooking and keep kitchen doors closed from living areas.

## 2. Zero-Cost Clean Air Hacks
1. **Damp Towel Draft Stops**: Place moist rolled towels along external door bottoms and drafty window sills (cuts particle infiltration by up to 40%).
2. **Designate a Clean Air Sanctuary**: Seal the smallest bedroom in the home where family spends 8 hours resting.
3. **Shoe-Free Home**: Remove outdoor footwear at the entry mat to prevent tracking lead, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), and fine street soot indoors.
4. **Timing Natural Airing**: Only open windows between 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM when solar heating disperses the inversion layer.
