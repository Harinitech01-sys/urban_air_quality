# Knowledge Base: Protecting Vulnerable Populations

## 1. Children & Adolescents
- **Physiological Factors**:
  - Higher metabolic rate and minute ventilation per kg of body mass (1.5x higher than adults).
  - Developing lungs with ongoing alveolar growth until age 8-10.
  - Obligate mouth-breathers during physical activity, bypassing nasal turbinate filtering.
- **Action Thresholds**:
  - AQI > 150: Move physical education (PE) classes, soccer, and outdoor sports indoors.
  - AQI > 250: Restrict outdoor recess; shift to quiet indoor activities.
  - AQI > 350: Advise hybrid or remote learning; cancel all extracurricular outdoor events.

## 2. Asthmatics, COPD & Cardiac Patients
- PM2.5 triggers bronchial spasms, neutrophil infiltration, systemic vascular inflammation, and elevated thrombotic risk within 2 to 6 hours of peak exposure.
- **Precautions**:
  - Maintain rescue inhalers (e.g. Salbutamol / Albuterol) on hand at all times.
  - Check peak expiratory flow (PEF) readings daily.
  - Avoid any morning cold-air exertion.
  - Emergency warning signs: Chest heaviness, inability to finish a full sentence in one breath, stridor, cyanosis (blue lips/fingers) $\rightarrow$ Seek immediate emergency medical care.

## 3. Elderly (65+)
- Vulnerable to fine particle-induced cardiac arrhythmias and cerebrovascular strokes.
- Replace traditional dawn walks with indoor walking or gentle chair stretches in a sealed room.
