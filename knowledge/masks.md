# Knowledge Base: Masks & Respirator Guidance

## 1. Classification & Filtration Ratings
- **N95 / FFP2 / KN95 / KF94**:
  - Filter standard: Minimum 95% efficiency against non-oily aerosols down to 0.3 microns (and higher for 2.5 microns).
  - Crucial requirement: **Airtight peripheral seal**. Facial hair (even 1-2 day stubble) degrades filtration efficiency from 95% down to 50-70%.
  - Metal nose clip must be molded with two hands over the bridge of the nose.
  - Reusability: Can be reused for 40 hours of cumulative wear if stored in a clean, breathable paper bag between uses and not physically soiled or deformed.
- **Surgical Masks**:
  - Loose fit with large perimeter leaks.
  - Typical fine particle (PM2.5) protection: Only 20% to 40%. Intended for outward droplet emission, not fine particulate inhalation protection.
- **Cloth & Bandanas**:
  - Porous woven fibers provide minimal filtration (< 15% against PM2.5). Do not rely on cloth masks during severe smog.

## 2. Strict Child & Infant Guidelines
- **Infants under 2 years old**: **NEVER WEAR MASKS**. Due to narrow airways and inability to communicate or remove the mask, masks present an acute suffocation hazard. Protect infants exclusively by keeping them in clean, sealed indoor environments.
- **Children aged 2 to 6**: Use pediatric-specific KF94 or small N95s that fit without gap. Never force if the child resists or displays labored breathing.
